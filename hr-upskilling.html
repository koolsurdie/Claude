YouTime — Dedicated to Your Development

Meeting-free learning blocks twice monthly to scale the HREE Summit and explore the learning methods that work best for you.

📅 Schedule

2nd & 4th Friday (MEAR/EUR) and Wednesday (APAC/AME) of the month — 1-hour blocks.

🎯 Focus

Prioritize HREE curated learning, or explore podcasts, communities, mentoring, and projects.

🏆 Leadership

Leaders champion continuous learning by setting the tone, integrating it into HREE values and strategy.

📊 Tracking

Calendar invites block time for all employees. Reporting reflects YouTime activity and learning uptake.
