Path 4 — Certification Details

Six high-commitment programs that contribute directly back to the business.

🎓 ICF Coaching Certification

€ 3,400 / participant*
  • 60 hours virtual training
  • 10 hours mentor coaching (2–3 days F2F)
  • 100 hours coaching experience (75:25 split)
  • Cohort-based, up to 12 participants
  • Full exam preparation support
  • Service agreement: up to 1 year
Prerequisites Min. 5 years HR experience, high empathy / EQ, understanding of coaching principles.

🔍 Insights Discovery Accreditation

€ 4,000 – € 6,000 / participant*
  • 5-day accreditation workshop (virtual or F2F)
  • Accredited for: Personal, Team, Sales, Leadership & Change Effectiveness
  • Commitment to facilitate 5 post-cert workshops
  • Service agreement: up to 1 year
Prerequisites 3–5 years facilitation experience, ability to analyse behavioural data, constructive feedback skills.

📋 PMP — Project Management Professional

€ 1,800 / participant*
  • On-demand PMP exam prep course
  • External instructor-led course option
  • Training and exam voucher (Q3 Quest)
Eligibility 36 months project leadership experience (past 8 yrs) + 35 hours PM education/training.

⚡ PMI-ACP — Agile Certified Practitioner

€ 1,800 / participant*
  • 21 hours agile practice training
  • External instructor-led prep option
  • Exam voucher available (Q3 Quest)
Eligibility 21 hours agile practice + 12 months general PM experience in last 5 years.

🚀 SAFe Certifications

Via LACE Team
  • SAFe Jumpstart (mandatory prerequisite)
  • Leading SAFe — for enterprise adoption leads
  • Cohort-based, facilitated by internal SPC teams
  • Strict qualification criteria apply
Note SAFe Jumpstart must be completed before Leading SAFe enrollment.

📜 ISO Certifications

€ 2,000 – € 4,000 / participant*
  • Multiple ISO certification options available
  • Selection in consultation with your manager
  • Contact: Luiz Vieira (Certification Team)
Process Manager approval required. Certification team facilitates enrollment and training logistics.
